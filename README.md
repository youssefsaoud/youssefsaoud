### 👨‍💻 A little about me

I'm a backend developer who enjoys turning ideas into applications and understanding the engineering decisions behind them.

My main focus is the **Java and Spring ecosystem**, especially designing REST APIs, working with relational databases, implementing authentication and authorization, and writing maintainable code.

I believe the interesting part of programming isn't just finding a solution. It's understanding why that solution works, what trade-offs it introduces, and how it could be improved.

- 🔭 **Currently building:** Orqestra, a project and vendor management platform.
- 🧠 **Interested in:** Backend architecture, APIs, application security, and database design.
- 🛠️ **Also exploring:** Python automation and developer tooling.
- ✍️ **Writing about:** What I build, what goes wrong, and what I learn along the way.
